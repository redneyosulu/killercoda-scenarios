# Back Up Fixture Data and Restore It to a Wiped Target

**Environment.** Ubuntu 24.04, About 50 minutes.

## What you will do

- Back up fixtures with recorded schema and context
- Restore to a separate wiped target, never over the original
- Verify with checksums and time the restore against an RTO

_Wait until the terminal prints `Environment ready` before step 1. Setup runs in the background._
