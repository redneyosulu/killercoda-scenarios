# Narrow an Over-privileged Service User and Rotate Its Secret

**Environment.** Ubuntu 24.04, About 45 minutes.

## What you will do

- Scope the identity to the minimum paths and commands its job needs
- Rotate the fixture credential with the correct response order
- Prove the job works and the old reach is denied
